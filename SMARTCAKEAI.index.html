Why this score

Weighted components and decision sensitivity

Score breakdown

Each component is scored 0–100, then weighted. Safety is also a hard gate: the decision cannot be GO until it is passed, and a failure means REJECT regardless of score.

Weights: compound reduction 20 · protein recovery 20 · cost 15 · sensory 15 · resources 10 · safety 15 · evidence 5. Outputs rely on illustrative assumptions until replaced with measured data.

What would change my decision?

Each input is varied ±20% and the SmartCake score and decision are recomputed. Ranked by influence.

Validation checklist

Decision rulePrefer the lowest-cost pathway that meets safety, protein-retention and sensory acceptance constraints—not simply the highest protein percentage.
Protein concentration can rise because non-protein mass is removed; that does not automatically mean protein mass or recovery improved. Track both concentration and mass balance.

Why this treatment?

Makes the Evidence-Guided Intelligence Engine explainable: the barrier, the treatment capability and the trade-offs behind the recommendation.
